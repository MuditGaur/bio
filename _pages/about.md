I am a **Postdoctoral Scholar at the AI-EDGE Institute**, in Electrical and Computer Engineering at **The Ohio State University**. I completed my **Ph.D. in Statistics at Purdue University in December 2025**, advised by [Vaneet Aggarwal](https://engineering.purdue.edu/IE/people/ptProfile?resource_id=119657) and [Raghu Pasupathy](https://www.stat.purdue.edu/people/faculty/pasupath.html).

My research develops statistical and optimization foundations for **adaptive learning and decision-making**. I am particularly interested in theoretical reinforcement learning, bilevel optimization and statistical inference for decision problems, robust and continual learning, and convergence guarantees for generative models. A recurring theme is the reliability of learning procedures when data, function approximation, and computational effort are limited.

## Research

- **Reinforcement learning with function approximation:** Finite-sample and convergence guarantees for fitted Q-iteration, actor–critic methods, and bilevel reinforcement learning with neural networks.
- **Statistical inference for bilevel decisions:** Estimation and uncertainty quantification for policy-mediated effects and learned responses, with connections to repeated-measure interventions.
- **Robust and continual reinforcement learning:** Reliable policy learning under uncertain dynamics, changing environments, and finite computational resources.
- **Generative models:** Statistical and optimization guarantees for diffusion models, discrete diffusion, and flow matching.
