Teaching approach

My teaching emphasizes connections among mathematical reasoning, computation, and substantive applications. I use small simulations and data-driven assignments to help students understand assumptions and interpretation, and I place particular importance on making statistical reasoning and communication accessible to students with different levels of mathematical or programming preparation.
